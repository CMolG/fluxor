# Product Hunt's launch guide, distilled

This is the rulebook the audit is scored against. It condenses Product Hunt's
official Launch Guide (nine pages under producthunt.com/launch), the Featuring
Guidelines help article, and Product Hunt's own posts. Wording in quotation
marks is Product Hunt's; everything else is a faithful paraphrase.

**Freshness.** Compiled in September 2026. Product Hunt changes limits and
mechanics from time to time; items marked *(volatile)* have changed before or
are stated differently in different official places. When you can reach
producthunt.com, spot-check those items; if you can't (some networks block it),
use the values here and say which ones you could not re-verify. Never work
around a network block with caches, mirrors or proxy services.

## 1. How Product Hunt works
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/how-product-hunt-works>

- Makers post products; the community upvotes, comments and shares them, and
  launches compete on a **daily leaderboard** for top spots, including
  **Product of the Day** (also Week and Month).
- A launch day runs on **Pacific Time, from 12:01 AM to 11:59 PM PT**.
- The homepage shows **Featured** products by default; everything else is only
  visible under the "All" toggle. "Every day, hundreds of products are
  submitted, but only a select few are featured on the homepage." Featuring is
  an editorial decision (see §10) — which is why featuring eligibility is the
  single most important thing this audit protects.
- The leaderboard "changes throughout the day based on the number of upvotes,
  comments, time since submission, and other factors". The exact algorithm is
  not disclosed "because doing so would make it easier to be gamed".
- Top products also reach Product Hunt's daily newsletter, and Product Hunt's
  writers cover launches — "great content, like a thoughtful first comment,
  goes a long way in giving our writers the tools they need to tell your story".
- Using Product Hunt (posting included) is free and needs a personal account.

## 2. Before launch
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/before-launch>

- **Account age:** "Newly created accounts will need to wait 1 week before
  posting a product" *(volatile)*. Use that week to learn the community.
- **Be part of the community first.** Product Hunt values engagement from
  seasoned members over newcomers. Comment on and support launches you
  genuinely like; complete your profile (the first comment's author profile is
  visible to everyone).
- **Hunters are optional.** Makers are encouraged to hunt their own products;
  Product Hunt reports that 79% of featured posts were self-hunted and 60% of
  #1 Product of the Day winners were self-hunted. A hunter can still help with
  feedback and reach, but is not required.
- **Schedule in advance.** Launches can be scheduled ahead (up to about a
  month *(volatile)*) and a Coming Soon / teaser page collects followers, who
  are notified when you go live. Not scheduling in advance is one of Product
  Hunt's listed ways to not get featured (§10).
- **Choosing a day.** Weekdays bring more traffic and more competition (larger
  companies launch on weekdays); weekends suit smaller teams, side projects
  and personal apps. Product Hunt's bottom line: the best day to launch is
  when you're ready.
- **Know your goal** (feedback, first users, press, investors) so you can
  measure the launch.

## 3. Preparing for launch — the listing
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch>

| Field | Rule | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | **≤ 40 characters** | Just the product name. |
| Tagline | **≤ 60 characters** | "A very short description of the product" with no gimmicks or over-the-top language. Say what it does / how it helps. No emoji, no hype. |
| Links | Primary website link + optional extra links | App Store, Google Play, Chrome Web Store, GitHub… Fewer choices convert better. Product Hunt appends `?ref=producthunt` to outbound links. |
| Description | **≤ 500 characters** *(volatile — older help articles said 260)* | What's new or different, which features stand out. Only about the first 250–260 characters show before truncation, so the core message must fit there. |
| Launch tags | **Up to 3** existing topics | Pick the most specific relevant topics. |
| Thumbnail | **Square, 240×240 recommended**; JPG/PNG/GIF | Animated GIFs play on hover. Keep it small (the form has capped thumbnails at 2–3 MB *(volatile)*). |
| Gallery | **At least 2 images required**, 3+ recommended; **1270×760 recommended**; each under 3 MB *(volatile)* | Show the real product — no stock images or marketing fluff. The **first image is the social preview** when the launch link is shared. Social proof and a closing call-to-action image are welcome. |
| Video | Optional YouTube or Loom link | Demo or maker story. |
| Interactive demo | Optional | Arcade, Storylane, Hexus, Supademo, Layerpath or ScreenSpace — free for Product Hunt launches. |
| Makers | Tag everyone who built it | Tagged makers get a Maker badge on their comments. |
| First comment | Strongly expected | "A highly visible part of the page" — explain the product, the makers, the story and the feedback you want. Only the first ~800 characters show before "see more" *(volatile)*. |
| Shoutouts | **Up to 3** products you use | Your launch is listed on their product pages; the form says launches with shoutouts are more likely to be featured on the homepage and in the newsletter. |
| Pricing | One of **Free · Paid · Paid (with a free trial or plan)** | Must match reality. |
| Promo code | Optional | Offer, code and expiration date must **all** be filled for a valid offer. |

You can still edit the post and change the launch date before launch
(`/edit` on the post URL).

## 4. Sharing your launch
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch>

- Share the launch link however you like: social posts, email to your users
  and newsletter, communities you belong to, personal messages. Personal
  outreach "goes a long way".
- **Do not ask for upvotes. Do not reward upvotes.** Ask people to visit, try
  the product, comment, give feedback or share it with someone relevant.
  Asking for votes "can get you in trouble and makes the launch look cheap",
  and the ranking algorithm penalises it.
- **Upvote services, vote exchanges and promotion/upvote groups** are
  prohibited: "Utilizing upvote services will result in your removal from the
  homepage", such groups produce spam activity that hurts the launch, and "if
  your 'growth consultant' is using prohibited tactics you will be held
  responsible".
- Don't spam: no mass unsolicited DMs or emails, no dropping links into
  communities you aren't part of.
- Product Hunt provides embeds and badges for your site, including a launch-day
  embed with live upvotes.

## 5. Launch day duties
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-duties>

- "If you have no constraint and want the full leaderboard day, launch at
  12:01 am Pacific Time."
- Post the maker **first comment** right away.
- "Product Hunt rewards active participation. Stay engaged." Reply to comments
  throughout the day; conversations bring feedback and often networking,
  partnership and funding opportunities.
- Prepare content beforehand so launch day is for engagement. A launch-day
  dashboard is available by adding `/launch-day` to the post URL.

## 6. Launch day questions
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/launch-day-questions>

- **Early hours are shuffled.** For the first hours of the day upvote counts
  are hidden and the leaderboard order is randomised to level the field
  *(volatile — described as the first 2 hours, 00:01–02:00 PT, on the FAQ and
  as the first 4 hours elsewhere; Product Hunt has also trialled "Randomized
  Leaderboard Days" that cycle randomised and ranked periods all day)*.
- **Sudden drops** in rank usually mean spam was detected in upvotes and/or
  comments and removed.
- **Not featured?** Most products are not featured on their first attempt.
  Product Hunt allows relaunching after six months, or sooner with a
  significant upgrade (§8).
- If two launches from the same company land on the same day, Product Hunt may
  reschedule one.

## 7. Days after launch
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/days-after-launch>

- Keep monitoring the launch page and replying to new comments as on launch
  day; keep sharing the link — Product of the Week and Month are still open.
- Follow up with the people who tried the product (reply to their comments,
  email them if you captured an address). Be sincere and humble, thank them,
  and remind new users of your Product Hunt offer if you had one.
- Add Product Hunt's badge or award embed to your site.

## 8. Two weeks post-launch
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/two-weeks-post-launch>

- "You can promote your Launch Page for two weeks after launch day"; after
  that, move to your **Product Page** — "your permanent and comprehensive
  source of truth". Claim it if you haven't.
- Keep collecting reviews and posting updates (Product Forums).
- **Relaunching:** wait at least **six months** between posts for the same
  product or from the same company, and have a **significant update**. Sooner
  requires a relaunch request; the system asks what changed.

## 9. Definitions
<https://www.producthunt.com/launch/definitions>

- **Hunter** — the person who posts (hunts) a product; may or may not be a maker.
- **Maker** — someone who built the product; tagged makers comment with a Maker badge.
- **Launch page** — the page created when a product is posted: details, makers, upvotes, comments.
- **Product page (hub)** — the permanent page gathering all launches, reviews and discussion of a product.
- **First comment** — the first comment on the launch page, shown right below the gallery, so it is highly visible. Product Hunt notes that about 70% of Product of the Day/Week/Month winners had a first comment posted by a maker.
- **Upvote** — how members support products they like.
- **Featured** — launches Product Hunt shows on the homepage by default.
- **Product of the Day / Week / Month** — awards for top-ranked launches.
- **Launch tags (topics)** — categories a launch is filed under.
- **Shoutouts** — products a maker credits for helping build theirs.
- **Product Forums (/p/)** — per-product community threads for updates and discussion.

## 10. Featuring guidelines
Help Center "Product Hunt Featuring Guidelines" and Product Hunt's own posts.

Featured launches are chosen manually against four criteria; a launch doesn't
need to excel at all four — it often spikes in one or two:

- **Useful** — practical, solves a real problem.
- **Novel** — new or meaningfully different.
- **High craft** — well designed, polished, delightful to use.
- **Creative** — imaginative or engaging.

"Engagement isn't everything": a launch with many upvotes can still miss
Featured. Product Hunt focuses on digital products that are **available now**:
unfinished or waitlisted products, vaporware and closed betas are not
featured; blog posts, lists and plain documents "dilute the homepage".

Product Hunt's official list of **ways to ensure you AREN'T featured** (X, March 2024):
1. Not a real product (e.g. a PDF guide)
2. No maker comment ("ALWAYS ADD THIS")
3. Your link is broken ("this happens more than you think")
4. You didn't schedule in advance ("ALWAYS DO THIS")
5. Duplicate launch ("don't launch it 3x")
6. Minor feature ("no small boy stuff")
7. Unclear tagline ("Don't say 'The best ever', say how the product helps people")

## 11. Community-observed patterns (not official rules)
Use these as supporting evidence only, and label them as observations in reports.

- Non-featured launches often get under ~100 visitors; featured ones get thousands.
- Launches without a free plan, free trial or instantly usable demo seem to struggle to get featured.
- Template-looking sites and near-duplicate layouts are often skipped.
- Upvotes from brand-new accounts are heavily discounted; supporters should have real, active accounts well before launch day (many teams ask supporters to sign up about a month ahead).
- Early momentum matters: the top four positions sit above the fold.

## 12. Known conflicts between sources
| Topic | Values seen | How the audit treats it |
|---|---|---|
| Description limit | 500 (current launch guide/form) vs 260 (older help article) | FAIL above 500; WARN above 260 because only ~260 chars show. |
| Gallery size | 1270×760 (launch guide) vs 1200×630 "optimized" (form hint seen in 2024–25) | Recommend 1270×760 with key content centred so either crop works. |
| Image file size | Thumbnail 2 MB vs 3 MB; gallery 3 MB vs 10 MB | Recommend ≤ 2 MB thumbnail and ≤ 3 MB gallery images. |
| Hidden-upvote window | first 2 hours vs first 4 hours | Say "the first hours"; the advice (ignore rank, focus on conversation) is the same. |
