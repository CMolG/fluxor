# Ledgerly: internal notes (from the founder)

## What it is
Invoicing + automatic payment chasing for freelancers and small agencies (1–10 people).
You connect Stripe or your bank, create an invoice in about a minute, and Ledgerly sends
polite reminder emails on a schedule you choose (e.g. 3 days before due, on the due day,
7 days late) until the invoice is paid. Clients pay by card or bank transfer from a link.
"AI" = it drafts the reminder wording in your tone and suggests the best send time. Honestly
the reminders are the thing people care about.

## Numbers (as of Sept 2026)
- 1,240 paying customers, ~3,100 free accounts. (The "10,000+" on the site is total sign-ups ever, including people who never sent an invoice.)
- Median time from due date to paid: 31 days before Ledgerly → 11 days after 3 months of use (internal analysis of 412 customers who imported past invoices, Jan–Jun 2026).
- 30+ currencies via Stripe.
- Integrations: Stripe, Wise, QuickBooks Online, Xero, Google Sheets export. No Zapier yet.
- Security: data hosted in the EU (Frankfurt), SOC 2 Type I done in March 2026 (Type II in progress). Card data never touches our servers (Stripe).

## Pricing
- Free: up to 3 invoices/month, reminders included, Ledgerly branding on invoices.
- Solo: $12/month (or $120/year): unlimited invoices, your branding, custom reminder schedule.
- Studio: $29/month (or $290/year): up to 10 team members, client portal, QuickBooks/Xero sync.
- 14-day trial of paid plans, no card needed. Cancel anytime. We do refunds on request within 30 days (we've never refused one).
- No sales calls needed: people sign up themselves. The demo form on the site is a leftover from when we tried enterprise.
