# progressive-disclosure: Load Last, Prove Early

A page moves from the value proposition to the proof in an order the reader can follow, and it reveals its weight a section at a time. Each section gives the reader one reason to keep going.

## Rules

1. **Value first, mechanism second, proof third.** The first screen says what the reader gets. Detail and interactive proof come after the reader has the point.
2. **Minimal load on arrival.** Above the fold, show one headline, one supporting line and one action. Anything else waits for a scroll or a click.
3. **Alternate the pattern.** Consecutive sections use different layout patterns, chosen from: an interactive hero sandbox, a bento grid, a before-and-after slider, an animated workflow, an ROI calculator. The same pattern never appears twice in a row.
4. **Interactive proof earns its place.** A demonstration is shown where it answers a question the reader has at that point, not as decoration.
5. **Stack with cognitive-ux.** Where both mods apply, the load budget of `cognitive-ux` holds and this mod sets the order within it.
6. **Keep the action in view.** The path to the one action stays reachable from every section.

## Output

A sequence of sections, each with its pattern, its job and the reader question it answers.
